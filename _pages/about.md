## About Me

My area of research is machine learning approaches in visual perception and understanding. It involves computer vision, natural language processing, and artificial intelligence. My work lies at the intersection of these areas where I develop applications that enable computers to read and understand textual data, process visual input such as object and scene recognition in images, and associate their "visual" and "reading" outputs.

Currently, I am more inclined towards research related to large language models, image recognition and multilingual artificial intelligence systems. The main reason behind this is the desire to make all these technologies practical and scalable, i.e., to develop systems which would prove effective and accessible for solving various problems of our time.


## Research Interests

My research interests are several key areas within Natural Language Processing and AI:

- **Deep Learning**
- **Neural Networks**
- **Computer Vision**
- **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**
- **Large Language Models (LLMs)**
- **Generative AI**
- **Responsible and Ethical AI**
- **Multimodal Learning**
